## Comments and the review cycle

On a site served with comments, sign in with the site password and your name, or with ORCID on a hub that offers it. Then:

- Select any text for Comment, Edit (just that passage) or a one-click tag: a request for the next run (explain, expand, shorten, delete, challenge, needs source) or the manuscript section the passage belongs in.
- The buttons at the bottom right act on the whole page: Comment; ✓ Approve on a page that waits for your sign-off; Change ▸ (Edit, Revise, Drop); More ▸ (Propose the next step of the reasoning for this kind of page: a hypothesis on a question, a caveat on a result, a control on a step, …; Next run; Manuscript). Every button records a request; none starts Claude.
- The pencils in the left margin edit the title or summary in place; the pencil after a section heading edits that section. Every edit is saved as a suggestion, shown as a diff, and goes into the next plan.

Nothing changes until the comments are harvested. A round has four steps, shown at the top of Progress and the plan page with the current one marked, and named in the menu bar:

1. Review. Readers comment, tag, edit, propose and sign off for as long as the round takes. Claude does nothing: the agent waits (`kea watch`) without reading anything; a long round costs nothing.
2. Harvest. A PI presses Harvest. The agent writes the sign-offs onto the pages, then folds every comment into one plan of numbered items (what will change, on which pages, the version it will make) and answers each comment with its item. For every stage that is now signed, the next step on the route becomes an item too ("Next step: run the plan of Q1"). A harvest runs nothing else. The plan stands until the next harvest; the plan page counts the comments made since, and "Harvest again" adds them.
3. Implement. A PI reads the plan and presses Implement. Comments pause. The agent does what the plan lists, and nothing more: it changes the pages item by item, takes the next steps listed (writing what it is doing on Progress as it goes), checks every number against its file, works through the rechecks, commits each change and answers each comment with its commit: page changed, answered, or won't fix with the reason.
4. Release. A new version and git tag, with the run's change log. Open pages reload and comments reopen. Comments left out of the plan stay for the next cycle. The History page links each comment to its plan item, commit and pages. Plan → Current comments lists who commented what and where, with a filter per person.

Signing off. On a page that waits for you, "✓ Approve & next →" signs that page only, bound to the version you read, and opens the next page that waits, top down through the tree; ‹ n of N › moves between them without signing. Your sign-off is recorded at once and the page says "✓ Signed · waits for Harvest"; nothing else happens until a PI presses Harvest. Progress lists the pages to sign in the same order, with "Start signing at the top".

Progress, in the menu bar (with the number of pages that wait for a sign-off), is the overview: the four steps of the round with the current one marked and one line on what happens now and what comes next; Needs your sign-off (each page, what to press, and the step the next plan will then hold); Claude's next steps (signed pages whose next step is Claude's: they go into the plan at the next Harvest and run at Implement); the route of every question (approved, plan, run, results, conclusions: done, waiting for you, Claude's turn, running, closed); and every sign-off with its name, version and date.

The plan page shows the same four steps and the agent's session (watching, with the time since its last heartbeat; working; ended; stopped unexpectedly), and offers Harvest and Implement to PIs. The menu bar names the step for every reader; PIs get the button that moves the round on: Harvest · N when comments and sign-offs wait, ▶ Implement once the plan stands (with ⇣ Harvest again · N beside it when comments came in after the plan; Progress then marks the round as back in Review and the plan as out of date), and what Claude is doing while it works. The plan page keeps the other controls (Harvest again, Cancel).
