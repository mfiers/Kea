## Comments and the review cycle

On a site served with comments, sign in with the site password and your name, or with ORCID on a hub that offers it. Then:

- Select any text for Comment, Edit (just that passage) or a one-click tag: a request for the next run (explain, expand, shorten, delete, challenge, needs source) or the manuscript section the passage belongs in.
- The buttons at the bottom right act on the whole page: Comment; on a page that waits for you, ▶ Approve & the next step (the main button) and ✓ Approve only (Confirm on a settled page); Change ▸ (Edit, Revise, Drop); More ▸ (Propose the next step of the reasoning for this kind of page: a hypothesis on a question, a caveat on a result, a control on a step, …; Next run; Manuscript).
- The pencils in the left margin edit the title or summary in place; the pencil after a section heading edits that section. Every edit is saved as a suggestion, shown as a diff, and goes into the next plan.

Nothing changes until the comments are harvested:

1. Comment. Readers comment, tag, edit and propose for as long as the round takes. The agent waits (`kea watch`) without reading them; comments alone never wake it, so a long round costs nothing.
2. Harvest. A PI presses "Harvest comments". The agent folds every comment into one plan of numbered items (what will change, on which pages, the version it will make) and answers each comment with its item. Sign-offs, and Revise and Drop on pages that wait for one, are not part of this cycle: the agent takes them up at once (see below). The plan stands until the next harvest; the plan page counts the comments made since, and "Harvest again" adds them.
3. Implement. A PI presses "Implement the plan". Comments pause. The agent changes the pages item by item, checks every number against its file, works through the rechecks, commits each change and answers each comment with its commit: page changed, answered, or won't fix with the reason.
4. Release. A new version and git tag, with the run's change log. Open pages reload and comments reopen. Comments left out of the plan stay for the next cycle. The History page links each comment to its plan item, commit and pages. Plan → Current comments lists who commented what and where, with a filter per person.

Sign-offs do not wait for a harvest. On a page that waits for you, the main button is ▶ Approve & the next step: it signs every page that waits in the same stage ("▶ Approve all 3 & run the plan") and asks the agent to take that stage's next step now (propose questions, write the plan, plan its steps, run the step, run the plan, draw conclusions). ✓ Approve only signs (the stage, or this page only) and starts nothing; the agent writes the sign-off onto the pages within a minute or two while a session watches. Revise and Drop on a waiting page are taken up the same way. On a page that is signed off already, or needs no sign-off, the button is just ▶ Run the step (or Write the plan, …): it asks for the step without adding a signature; a second reader signs with Approve. It needs the iteration password, like Harvest. A line at the top of every page then says what was asked, whether a session is watching, and, once the agent has picked it up, what it is doing ("running S2: 3 of 8 donors"); the page reloads when the new version is published. The agent takes only that one step and stops for your review.

Progress, in the menu bar (with the number of pages that wait for a click), is the overview. Waiting for you lists every page that waits for a reader, in one table: a page that needs your sign-off (Approve signs it off and stops there) and a page that is signed off, or needs no sign-off, whose next step is Claude's but not started (▶ Run the step, or the step named, asks for it); each row names the button and the step it starts. Below: the route of every question (approved, plan, run, results, conclusions: done, waiting for you, the agent's turn, running, closed), the open Approve & go requests with the agent's status, and every sign-off with its name, version and date.

The plan page shows the agent's session (watching, with the time since its last heartbeat; working; ended; stopped unexpectedly) and offers Harvest and Implement to PIs. PIs (readers who may steer) also get one button in the menu bar that follows the cycle: Harvest · N when comments wait outside the plan, ▶ Implement once the plan stands, and what Claude is doing while it works; the plan page keeps the other controls (Harvest again, Cancel).
