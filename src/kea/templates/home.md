Executive summary: what the experiment asks, what it found and how sure that is, in a few sentences for a reader
who knows the field but not this project. (Everything before the first `##` heading is the summary; the site puts
every question with its answer in short below it, and the PDF opens with it.)

## Scope

What is in and out, the data, and whether the experiment is exploratory or confirmatory. Add question pages under
`question/` (each with an `answer:` of two or three sentences once it is answered), premises under `premise/`,
hypotheses (with `fixed_before_results`) under `hypothesis/`; see `doc/_schema.yaml` for all types and relations.
