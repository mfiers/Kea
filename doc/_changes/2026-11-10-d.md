## Asked

Mark Fiers, 10 November 2026, through the s100 session: a readability review of the s100 site (an independent reader, build of s100 v0.12.0 on Kea 1.23.1), nine points for Kea; in chat: implement them, release, and have the hub and s100 update.

## Changed

- A conclusion that names its question (a question as parent, or `answers:`) is no longer also listed under another question whose results it cites. On s100, C2 was shown as Q2's conclusion in At a glance and on the home page; it now sits under Q3 and Q4 only.
- A result's figures are shown in full right under its summary, on the site and in the PDF: the image, the figure's tag and take-home once, the rest of the caption folded ("Shows, method and statistics"). `![[f-…]]` on a line of its own places a figure inside the text instead. Question and conclusion pages show the first figure of each result behind them as a strip of small images.
- The take-home is printed once: on figure pages the caption no longer repeats the summary box, and in the PDF the caption's bold first line is left out where the summary above says it.
- "Changes to this page" is one line per version (release, date, what changed, links to the commits); the diffs stay on the commit pages under History.
- Inline data links are short chips (the name given, or the file name) with the path on hover, not long monospace paths.
- Under the text, conclusions, caveats, premises, references and terms linked to a page are one line each (tag, title, status, why the link holds) instead of a table; steps, resources, artefacts, decisions and sources fold into one Provenance block.
- A dataset id (a resource alias such as G23/MIC) is followed by the dataset's name at its first mention in a page's prose.
- `kea build` makes two PDFs: the reader's copy (the story, the glossary and the references; s100: 68 pages) and the complete one with the records (139 pages); the menu's PDF opens the reader's copy, "With all records" the complete one. Links to pages only in the complete copy fall back to their text. A table of seven or more columns in a page's text gets a landscape page.
- The help, README and skill describe the above.

## Not done

- The review's "wide tables print tiny (p.21)": s100's page tables have at most six columns, and its page 21 (complete PDF) holds no table; the landscape rule applies from seven columns. Ask the reviewer which table was meant if it persists.
