## Asked

Mark Fiers, 20 November 2026: show the keyboard shortcuts in the hovers, for example on Approve & next, prefixed with the key, and make that hover a bit shorter.

## Changed

- Buttons and menu-bar links with a key show it in front of their hover text: the Approve button a n (or a a) and, once signed, a d; c Comment; a p Edit, a r Revise, a x Drop, a + Propose, a w Next run, a m Manuscript; N / P the sign-off order; the Harvest button s h (menu bar and plan page) and Implement s i; g s, g m, g l, g o, g t, g b, g d, g k, g r, g p, g c, g w, g h, g f, g ? on the menu bar's links; 1 to 9 on the tabs. The keys stay in front when the page scripts rewrite a hover (kea.js `keyHints`, a MutationObserver).
- The Approve hover is shorter: "a n · Sign off on this page (version …), then open S1 …. Written onto the page at the next Harvest; nothing starts. a a: this page only."
- Help (Reading a page) and README updated.

Hub: upgrade not needed (page scripts only).
