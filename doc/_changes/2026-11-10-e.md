## Asked

Mark Fiers, 10 November 2026: on question, conclusion and result pages show the page's own content first (title, summary, text), then a question's answer so far, then figures, tables and attachments under their own separator in the style of "About this page" (tabbed when they take much space, the tabs on the separator), then About this page.

## Changed

- Figures and data files moved from under the summary to after the text (and a question's "Answer so far"), under a separator like "About this page" labelled Figures, or Figures and files. With more than one item the separator carries one tab per figure or file (tag and title; the summary on hover), one panel shown at a time; printing shows every panel.
- The section holds the page's figures in full (take-home, the rest folded), the strip of the results' first figures on question and conclusion pages ("From the results"), and the page's data files with preview and download (`from_artefact:`, artefacts under it, artefact files named in `sources:`); these are no longer listed again under About this page.
- `![[f-…]]` still places a figure inside the text. The PDF is unchanged (a result's figures follow its summary).

`pytest` (114 tests; the figure test now checks the order text → Figures → About this page). s100 built from a scratch copy; result page with two figure tabs and question page looked at in screenshots.
