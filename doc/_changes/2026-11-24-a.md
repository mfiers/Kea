## Asked

Mark Fiers, 24 November 2026: the layout and structure are too complicated. Keep the graph and its pages, but make the main view one large graph in a horizontal tree layout, scrollable, walked with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse, each page coloured and lettered by type; a click or Space zooms into the page (as now, but more minimal) to comment and sign, Esc zooms back out, so that where you are in the experiment stays clear. Later: think about the scientific logic of working through the pages; be thorough and user friendly. Critical feedback from Fable (a second model) was asked for and used.

## Found

The pages, their relations and the sign-off machinery stay as they are; what was missing is one place that shows all of it in order. Fable's main points, taken: a single tree parent misrepresents a conclusion that rests on several results (so conclusions hang on their question and every supports / opposes edge into a conclusion is drawn, always); columns fixed by stage with a band per question rather than a centred tidy tree; every signable page reachable by the keys (caveats and decisions are full boxes, not chips); Esc inside the zoomed page must first close what is open there; n for the next page to sign; the address keeps the place. Building it turned up that `node.incoming` is keyed by the reverse label ("Shows"), not the relation; explore.py maps it (figures had fallen back to their question).

## Changed

- New page `explore.html` (Graph in the menu bar; `g g` on any page opens it at that page) and `_graph.json` (explore.py): every page with one tree parent (a hypothesis on its question, a step on the hypothesis it tests, a result on the step that made it, a figure on its result, a conclusion on its question; caveats on what they limit, decisions on where they are applied, premises on what relies on them, artefacts on the step that made them; resources, terms and references in a folded library band), its home question (whereami.py), its typed edges, what it rests on (graph.upstream) and its scientific state (fixed before results, exploratory or confirmatory, status, certainty, held after an upstream change).
- explore.js draws and walks it: columns by stage, a band per question, the experiment as a bar; → what follows, ← what it rests on, ↑ ↓ the same stage, PgUp/PgDn questions, n/p the pages that wait for a sign-off, / find, o/O fold, f fit, ? keys; a tip that names the page's links in words; an overview of the whole sheet; the selected page's chain of reasoning lit up (blue: rests on, orange: rests on it), followed along the route only.
- Space, Enter or a click zooms the page out of its box into a panel over the dimmed graph; the panel holds the site's own page without its menu bar, crumbs and strips (render.page marks it kea-embed), so comment, sign-off and the a / c keys work as on the page. Esc (kea.js, capture phase) closes the comment window, a menu or a field first, warns about an unsent comment, then zooms back. Approve & next and links inside the panel move the graph's selection with them. The graph's own page does not load the comment layer (feedback.js); cycle.js exposes keaPollStatus so the graph refreshes the sign-off marks when the panel closes, and shows an approve at once (dashed green).
- The pages themselves are unchanged apart from the Graph link and embed mode. Help (Reading) describes the graph.

Hub: upgrade not needed (static pages, a JSON file and page scripts; no endpoint, status field or publish format changed).
