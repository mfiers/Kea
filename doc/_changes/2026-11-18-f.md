## Asked

Mark Fiers, 18 November 2026: Progress says "needs your sign-off" although he signed off, with "asked: see the top of the page" under it, while the top of the page says "Claude is working: Signed off; starting: download of F26/PFC". Look at the Progress page critically.

## Found

- The Approve of an Approve & go was not counted as a sign-off: the server's `signing` list took only plain Approve threads, so the page kept "● needs your sign-off".
- For a page with an open Approve & go, Progress kept the build's label on purpose and added "asked: see the top of the page"; it never said that Claude had started.
- Only the page clicked counted as asked; the other pages on the request's `Signs:` line (a stage) still waited.

## Changed

- The server counts an open "Approve & …" request as a sign-off of every page on its `Signs:` line.
- Progress, live, one state per page: "✓ signed on the site" (Claude writes it on), "✓ signed · ▶ next step asked" (with whether Claude picks it up within a minute or waits for a session), "✓ signed · ⟳ Claude is working" (with Claude's last word from `kea progress`); the button column says "nothing to click", the count drops, the route's cell says signed.

## Checked

`pytest` (130 passed). Headless Chromium on `kea serve`, guided scratch project: an "Approve & run the plan" request with a three-page `Signs:` line: the banner, the three rows ("✓ signed · ▶ next step asked … waits for a Claude session"), the count 0 and the route cell agree; after `kea progress "Signed off; running S1: 3 of 8 donors" --page ex:h-a` the rows read "✓ signed · ⟳ Claude is working" with that line.
