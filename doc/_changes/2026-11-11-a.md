## Asked

Mark Fiers, 11 November 2026: the Kea and hub versions in the user menu rather than in the bar; the menu bar's fold-outs visually more distinct, and Map one level (no Map › Objects › Data/Code); on the experiment page, "Answers so far" without the conclusions' text (the question, its answer, a link to the conclusion); under "About this page", no resources, terms and references (into the Map fold-out, with consistent naming), only the straight links (hypotheses, steps, results, conclusions), and pageable; the page much too long: the separate headers as tabs, keeping the fat line with the tab hanging under it.

## Changed

- After a page's text, the sections hang as tabs under one separator in the type's colour: the open tab solid and hanging lower, the others pale, with counts. Experiment: Questions, Answers so far, About this page, Changes, Comments; other pages: About this page, Changes, Comments. Figures and files, and a question's answer, keep their own separator above. `#tab=<name>` opens a tab, a link to something in a closed tab opens it; in print and without scripts every panel shows. The permanent identifier opens the About tab. The s100 experiment page goes from 530 KB to 116 KB.
- Answers so far: each question's answer, then links to its conclusions with their status (no conclusion text).
- About this page lists no resources, terms or references (on their own pages they still list each other; a resource that made a page stays in its folded Provenance). The experiment's About lists its hypotheses, steps, results and conclusions in one table with a filter per kind; long tables of pages show 12 rows at a time with previous/next (`_assets/kea.js`).
- Menu bar: Map is one level: Relations, Objects, Resources, Terms, References (Objects filtered by type), Data files (was Data), Code. An open group's name turns amber and its items sit in a light strip.
- The Kea version pill left the bar: the reader's menu ends with the site's Kea, the site version and the server's or hub's Kea (amber when they differ); on a static site the Help link's hover says it.
- Help, skill and README describe the tabs, the menu and the versions.
