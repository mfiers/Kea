## Asked

Mark Fiers, 20 November 2026: a site-actions shortcut (s then h: Harvest) with a confirmation that the keyboard can accept, and other shortcuts of that kind; why g P and not g p; and for page navigation also neighbours: on a question g 1 to x, or next and previous with g n and g p.

## Changed

- **s** opens "This site …": h Harvest (or Harvest again), i Implement (only on the plan page, where its button is, below the plan), x cancel a harvest or implement request that has not started. Entries that do not apply now, or are for a PI only, are greyed out. Each opens a confirmation with the same words as the buttons; Enter or y confirms, Esc or n cancels. The requests are the buttons' own (cycle.js `post`).
- **g**: places on the site are small letters again (g p Plan, g m Map, g r Progress …, as in 1.32.3–1.32.6). The pages linked to this one moved to capitals, the letter of their number as the page shows it (g Q, g R, g W …), because twelve of the kind letters are also place letters. New "same kind" part: a number goes to that page of this page's kind in the experiment (g 3 on a question: Q3; g 1 2: Q12; on the experiment's front page, its questions), and ← / → go to the previous and next page of the same kind, also without g; a step bar shows where you are among them. g n / g p for this were not possible: p is the Plan.
- A key typed right after g, before the menu is up, is kept and used.
- Help (Reading a page) and README updated.

Hub: upgrade not needed (page scripts and CSS only; the cycle requests are the existing ones).
