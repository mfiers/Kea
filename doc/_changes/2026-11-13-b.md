## Asked

Mark Fiers, 13 November 2026: the cycle feels slow — clicking, and not much happens; better feedback on how to progress through an analysis (question → approve → define the steps → approve → execute). Then: distinguish "approve" from "approve & go". Approve stays important on pages that need a human's approval: take it as a signature, harvested, recording who signed off and on which version. Approve & go is the same, and also asks to proceed; the UI must say what that is ("write the plan", "run the step"). Keep the slowness, but give good feedback. And a global view (in the plan, or another view) of progress and of where feedback is required, at every level: approval of pages and approval of progress.

## Found

An approval was a comment thread: nothing happened until a PI pressed Harvest, the agent folded "Mark approved Q" into a plan, a PI pressed Implement, and only then did the agent write `approved:` and the next step. One step along the route took three clicks and two wake-ups, and the page said nothing in between.

## Changed

- **Approve** is a sign-off: `approved:` is now a list, one signature per person (`by`, `orcid`, `date`, `version`, `how`); an older single mapping still counts. The site sends the page's version id with every thread (`page_version`), so a signature names what the reader read. Signed pages carry a "✓ approved by …" pill whose hover lists names, versions and dates. Applied at the next harvest, as before; it asks for no further work.
- **Approve & go** (new tag `go`, needs the right to steer like Harvest): the same signature plus a request to take that page's next step now. The button names the step from the route (`graph.go_action`): propose questions (an experiment without questions), write the plan (a question without hypotheses or steps), plan its steps (a planned hypothesis no step tests), run the step (a planned step), draw conclusions (the last unreviewed result of a question without conclusions). It shows also on a page that is signed off already, when the next step is the agent's. Without the iteration password the site asks for it.
- `kea watch` stops with **exit 40** for an open Approve & go (oldest first) and tells the agent what to do. **`kea progress "…" --page <page>`** reports what the agent is doing (a heartbeat with `exit: going` and the text; on a hub through the lease heartbeat). `kea list` shows the page version a thread was made on.
- Feedback after the click: a line at the top of every page says what was asked, by whom and whether a session is watching, then "Claude is working: <its last kea progress>"; the button turns into "asked, waiting for Claude" (click to take it back) and then "⟳ Claude: …". Pages poll every 6 s while a request is open (20 s otherwise). `/api/status` carries the open requests (`goes`), the pages with a sign-off waiting for the harvest (`signing`), and the agent's `doing` and `page`.
- **Plan → Progress** (`progress.html`; the menu shows how many pages wait): the agent's status and the open Approve & go requests (live); the pages waiting for a sign-off with the button that moves each on; the pages ready for their next step; the route of every question (approved, plan, run, results, conclusions: done, waiting for you, the agent's turn, running, closed; "not needed" under pace: free); every sign-off with name, version, date and how. On a phone the lists become cards.
- Skill (pace, review cycle step 3 "Approve & go (exit 40)", tags), the CLAUDE.md section of `kea init`, help (Progress, Reviewing) and README describe both buttons, exit 40, `kea progress` and the Progress page.
- On phones the status banners no longer stick over the sticky menu bar.

## Checked

In a browser, on a throwaway guided project served with `kea serve`: the Approve and Approve & run the step buttons, the confirmation text with the version, the "asked, waiting" state and banner with no session watching, `kea watch` exiting 40, `kea progress` shown within seconds in the banner, on the button and on the Progress page, and the Progress page at desktop and 390 px width. ## Not done

Not tried on a hub (the hub runs the same server code; `kea progress` goes through the lease heartbeat). Existing projects keep their CLAUDE.md section (`kea init` adds it only when missing, never updates it): their agents learn exit 40 from the skill and from what `kea watch` prints when it stops; the section's new lines have to be copied in by hand. The agent writing the signature on an Approve & go is left to the agent (as for Approve at a harvest); nothing writes `approved:` automatically.
