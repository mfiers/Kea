## Asked

Mark Fiers, 18 November 2026: the workflow is still confusing; it is unclear when one is signing off and when Claude suddenly starts working. Go back to the old mode, with a clear distinction between harvest and working; make it crystal clear what needs signing off and what is waiting. Asked to choose, he chose: sign-offs are recorded at the harvest, and the next step of every signed stage becomes a plan item, done at Implement.

## Found

- Since 1.28, a page click could start Claude at once (Approve & go, `kea watch` exit 40), and since 1.30 any sign-off, Revise or Drop on a waiting page did too (exit 50). Readers could not tell from the buttons which click would start work and which would not; the banner, the Progress rows and the dock each described the request differently.

## Changed

- One rule: no click on a page starts Claude. A round has four steps: Review (readers comment and sign off; Claude does nothing), Harvest (a PI presses it; Claude writes the sign-offs onto the pages and writes the plan, with the next step of every signed stage as an item; it runs nothing), Implement (a PI presses it; Claude does what the plan lists and nothing more), Release.
- Removed: Approve & go and ▶ Run the step (the server refuses the `go` tag with an explanation), `kea watch` exits 40 and 50, the `route` tag, the request banner and the Approve & go table. Revise and Drop are plan items on every page. Old open `go` threads count as an Approve plus a request for the step, at the next harvest.
- Page buttons: Comment · ✓ Approve (all N, or this page only) · Change ▸ · More ▸. After the click the page says "✓ Signed · waits for Harvest"; the tooltip says which step the plan will then hold.
- Menu bar, for every reader: the step of the round ("Review · N for the plan", "⟳ Claude is writing the plan", "Plan ready", "⟳ Claude is working"); PIs get the button that moves it on (⇣ Harvest · N, ▶ Implement). The Progress count is the pages that need a sign-off, and drops live as pages are signed.
- Progress and the plan page: the four steps with the current one marked, and one line on what happens now and what comes next ("Now: Review. Claude does nothing. Waiting for the Harvest: 1 sign-off (3 pages). Next: …"). Progress has two tables: Needs your sign-off (what to press, and the step the next plan then holds) and Claude's next steps (signed pages: next Harvest → plan; runs at Implement). The route says "Claude's turn · next plan".
- `kea sign --pending` is the first step of a harvest; `kea progress` reports work during Implement. Skill, CLAUDE.md template, tag instructions and help rewritten to match.
