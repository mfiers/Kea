## Asked

## Found

- An Approve on the site is a thread in the comment log; the page's `approved:` is written by the agent (`kea sign --thread`) at the next harvest or Approve & go, and the static Progress page is only rebuilt then. Until then Progress said "● needs your sign-off" with a small grey line under it ("signed on the site; written onto the page at the next harvest"), the route said "● waiting for you", the count did not drop and the button column still offered Approve.
- "Approve all N" (one thread on one page, a `Signs:` line naming the others) counted only the page clicked: the other pages of the stage still showed "needs your sign-off" on Progress and "✓ Approve all N" in their own buttons.

## Changed

- The server's status lists every page on an open Approve's `Signs:` line as signed.
- Progress, live: a signed page says "✓ signed on the site" (with when Claude writes it on), its button column says "nothing to click", the count of pages waiting drops, and a route cell whose pages are all signed says so.
- On another page of a signed stage the button says "✓ Signed with its stage" (taking it back is done on the page where it was given).
