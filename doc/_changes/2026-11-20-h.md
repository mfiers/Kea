## Asked

Mark Fiers, 20 November 2026: "when I select text & get a popup - can we get shortcuts there as well?"

## Changed

- The popup that opens on selected text has keys, shown as a small badge on each button: c Comment, e Edit, and one letter per tag, the first letter of its label not yet taken, skipping a leading article ("a premise" gets r), else a digit. With the default tags: x explain, p expand, s shorten, d delete, h challenge, n needs source; r premise, y hypothesis, t step; i introduction, m methods, u results, o discussion, l conclusions. A key does what the click does (a tag is saved at once, with Undo).
- While the popup is open, a single key belongs to it: n tags "needs source" instead of going to the next page. Shift with the arrows still extends the selection, and Esc closes. The badges are hidden on touch screens.
- Help (Reading a page) mentions the keys.

Hub: upgrade not needed (page scripts only).

## Checked

`pytest` passed. Headless Chromium on a scratch guided project: selecting a paragraph showed the badges; n saved the tag "needs source" and stayed on the page; c opened the comment form; e opened the editor at the passage; without a selection, n went to the next page; no script errors.
