## Asked

Mark Fiers, 20 November 2026: ways to keep track of where you are in an experiment, starting with a consistent indication of the question you are in; ideas from a Fable session and mockups on a canvas. Chosen: the question as a tag only in the menu bar (variant A); the question in full in the breadcrumbs, with the current page as its tag only; the question strip; the corner thumbnail. "I like the current version, please give it a go."

## Changed

- Every page of an experiment gets a home question from the graph (`docsite/whereami.py`): a question is its own; the question's route (hypotheses, planned steps, results, conclusions) and the pages under a question belong to it; every other page takes the questions of the pages it is linked with, never through the experiment page, a resource, a term or a reference, and never across experiments. The parent question wins, then the question it is most tied to. A page tied to more than half of the questions (and at least three) belongs to the experiment as a whole. On trajectories: 114 of 116 pages get a question, D2 serves two, six shared resources are experiment-wide.
- Menu bar: the question's tag before the page's (Q1 › R1), the question on hover, "+1" when the page serves another one. The coloured bar on the left carries it at its top.
- Breadcrumbs: Start › experiment › the question in full › the page's tag. A caveat, decision or premise under the experiment gets its home question in that place.
- Question strip under the breadcrumbs: the question's route (H, S, R, F, C) and beside it the caveats, decisions and premises, each a tag link, filled when signed, outlined when not, ringed for this page; on the right where you are ("R1 · 4 of 9 · 1 not signed"). More than eight of a kind fold to a count, and on a phone every kind does.
- Corner plan: `kea build` writes `<experiment>/_where.json`; kea.js draws a small plan in the top right corner (one column per question, one row per stage, one dot per page: filled signed, outlined not, amber needing a look this round, a yellow ring for this page, sliding from the page you came from). Where the margin is too narrow it sits at the end of the strip. A dot opens its page; elsewhere on it, or **m**, opens the whole plan with every page's tag (arrows move, Enter opens, Esc or m closes).
- Help (Reading a page) and README updated.

Hub: upgrade not needed (page building, page scripts and a static `_where.json` per experiment, published with the site).
