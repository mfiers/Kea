## Asked

Mark Fiers, 9 November 2026: comments should not wake the agent by themselves, because folding them in after every burst costs many tokens in long review rounds. The Freeze button becomes Harvest; after a harvest the plan stands, and whoever may steer sees, before Implement, how many comments are not in it. Also: the site on a phone, where the page buttons cover the text, the editor cannot be used and tables are squeezed.

## Changed

- Review cycle (Kea 1.18.0): collecting → a steerer presses **Harvest comments** → the agent folds every comment into the plan (`kea cycle plan-updated` ends the harvest) → the plan stands until the next harvest → **Implement the plan**. `kea watch` exits 10 only on a harvest request, 20 on Implement; comments alone never wake it. Comments stay open everywhere until Implement.
- The plan page counts the comments not in the plan (made at or after the harvest request, unless the agent took them in) and offers **Harvest again (N)**. Implement no longer waits until every comment is in the plan: the confirmation names how many are left out, and they stay for the next cycle.
- Freeze and Unfreeze are gone (API `harvest` replaces `freeze`); old cycle logs replay, a frozen project reads as harvested.
- Phones (760 px or narrower): the page buttons start folded and fold again on scrolling or a tap elsewhere; the editor fills the screen without its explanation, with plain text fields of 16 px (no zoom on iOS, no highlighting layer that drifts); the comment panel takes the full width. Page tables become cards at 640 px or narrower.
- Help page, plan and cycles pages, README, the skill, CLAUDE.md and its template, and the project site describe the new cycle.

## Checked

`pytest` (87 tests; new: harvest transitions, the count after a harvest, a watcher that ignores comments, Implement with comments left out); the plan page in every state and the phone layout in headless Firefox screenshots.
