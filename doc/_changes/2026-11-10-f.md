## Asked

Mark Fiers, 10 November 2026: "Answer so far" sat on the left while the other separators sit on the right; the title, summary and text of a page in a different face, typewriter-like but more modern and friendly than Courier, indented, so the page as written stands out.

## Changed

- The "Answer so far" separator carries its label on the right, like "Figures" and "About this page".
- The title, summary and text of a page are set in IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Latin subset, copied into each site's `_assets/`, so sites still make no outside requests), indented behind a thin rule in a light tint of the page type's colour. Tables and figures in the text keep the sans-serif; the answer keeps the serif. Characters outside the subset, and sites opened from disk in Firefox (which blocks fonts on file:// pages), fall back to the system's monospace.
- Help: the page-order line says the page's own text is shown as written, in a typewriter face.

## Checked

`pytest` (114 tests). The s100 site rebuilt with this version and looked at in screenshots over http (Q3, R1): the label on the right, the face loaded (document.fonts: Kea Mono 400 and 600 loaded).
