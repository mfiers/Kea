## Asked

Mark Fiers, 9 November 2026, for the next release: is the graph checked for structural soundness in every iteration (a conclusion derives from results, not from hypotheses)? Are pages downstream of a change (a revised premise) critically re-assessed, in a compromised state until checked? A clearer overview of Kea's reasoning route and of how comments are implemented in Help. Proposals that follow the logical route on every page type, in the page menu next to Confirm and Reject. (The hub changes asked at the same time are in run 2026-11-09-hub.)

## Found

Neither was done. `kea build --strict` checked every link on its own (allowed source and target types), but no rule over the whole graph; the `required:` block in `_schema.yaml` ("a conclusion needs at least one result behind it") was never enforced. Nothing marked pages after an upstream change.

## Changed

- `kea check` and every build check the graph as a whole (docsite/graph.py). Problems: a conclusion resting on no result or figure (directly or through other conclusions); circular support; a conclusion that relies on a hypothesis or question; a live conclusion on deprecated evidence; a confirmatory conclusion on exploratory evidence only; a hypothesis with an outcome that no result tests. Notes: an answered question nothing answers, a result without a step, high certainty on exploratory evidence or under an open caveat, a supported premise with nothing behind it.
- Rechecks: each page depends on the pages upstream of it; `doc/_checks.yaml` keeps the fingerprint of each upstream page when the page was last checked. A change in substance marks the page and everything downstream with a red **recheck** pill, a gap in `kea context` and a note in `kea check`; `kea recheck <id> --because "…"` records the check; `kea cycle release` refuses while pages wait (`--allow-recheck "why"`). The first build records the baseline, so adopting this flags nothing.
- **Propose ▸** in the page menu (next to Confirm and Reject) and a Propose row over selected text: the next step of the reasoning for the page type (13 proposals: a question or premise on an experiment; a hypothesis, step or premise on a question; a step or competing hypothesis on a hypothesis; a control or other method on a step; a conclusion, caveat, control or other explanation on a result; a caveat, other explanation, follow-up question or certainty on a conclusion; why a premise may not hold; another option on a decision; a step that addresses a caveat). The reader writes the proposal (the server refuses one without text); it is harvested like a comment with an instruction for the agent.
- Help: "The reasoning route" (the main line from experiment to conclusion, what feeds into it, what Kea checks, what a recheck means) and "How a comment becomes a change" (comment → harvest → plan → implement with rechecks → release).
- The skill, README, CLAUDE.md and its template describe rechecks and proposals.

## Added later in the run: one step at a time

Asked: asked to formulate questions for an experiment, the agent went on and implemented them at once; the owner wants to read and review each step. Changed: the skill now has a pace rule (one step along the route, experiment → questions → hypotheses and planned steps → results → conclusions, then stop; further only when the owner says so in chat). Approval is recorded on the page (`approved:` from chat, or a Confirm on the site, applied at the harvest itself). With `pace: guided` in kea.yaml (written by `kea init`) `kea check` refuses hypotheses or steps under an unapproved question, a step running or done without approval and conclusions from unreviewed results; `kea context` starts with "Next step on the route". The question page shows the question's own text alone; the answer so far follows under its own rule with a note that it is a summary, not part of the question and not evidence. Tests: 98.

## Added later in the run: Approve or Confirm, Revise, Drop

Asked: is Approve the same as Confirm, and what does Reject mean (rewrite or remove)? Changed: the page verdicts are Approve · Revise ▸ · Drop on a page that waits for approval (pace: guided; a "waiting for approval" pill), applied at the next harvest so the step-by-step route never waits for an Implement, and Confirm · Revise ▸ · Drop on a settled page, done at Implement. Confirm is the human stamp (`confirmed:`); Revise needs the reader's text and reworks the branch (the page is rewritten, approval removed, everything downstream rechecked); Drop removes the page and every page that rests only on it: everything under it, and pages whose carriers (a conclusion's evidence, the step that made a result, the hypothesis a step tests, what a caveat limits) are all dropped; background (premises, decisions, resources) does not carry. The Drop button names those pages; `kea drop <id>` lists them for the agent. Reject (before 1.19) reads as Drop. Tests: 101.
