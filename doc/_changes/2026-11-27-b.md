## Asked

Mark Fiers, 27 November 2026, after his brief for trajectories went into the design round: is there now a batch sign-off? There is still a lot of clicking. Decision: build "Sign this design", per question only, and every signature it gives carries a flag that it was batch-signed. This overrides his rule of 18 November (one Approve signs one page, never a bundle) for a question's design only; results and conclusions are still reviewed one page at a time.

## Found

Under `pace: design` a whole experiment's design is drafted in one round, so it is easily 20 to 40 pages, each signed with its own Approve. The stage bundles of 1.29-1.31.0 had been removed because one click signed pages nobody had looked at. The mechanism they used (an Approve thread with a `Signs: page@version, …` line) is still read by the hub (pages shown as signed at once) and by `kea sign`, so the new button needs no hub change.

## Changed

- On a question's page under `pace: design`, while any page of its design waits: a banner at the top ("This question's design waits for your signature: N pages") and, after the question's text, "Sign this question's design": every waiting page of the design in full (title, summary, each signed field such as prediction and criterion, its links, its text), top down, the pages shared with other questions marked shared, and one button "Sign this design (N pages)".
- What one press signs (`graph.question_design`): the question, its live hypotheses and planned steps, and the experiment page, premises and decisions they rest on, as far as they still wait; never results or conclusions, never another question's own pages (those have their own button), nothing under `pace: guided` or `free`.
- The press re-reads the page first: when the design changed since it was opened, nothing is signed and the reader is asked to reload. Otherwise it saves one Approve on the question naming each page and the version shown; the hub shows all of them as signed at once (waits for the harvest), "Take it back" withdraws it.
- `kea sign --pending` (at the harvest) writes each signature with `batch: <question>`; the page's pill reads "✓ approved by … · batch: with Q1's design", the signature's description "signed in one go with the design of Q1". `kea check` reports a `batch:` mark that the thread does not back (a page signed alone, or a design of another question).
A review of the change (a second agent, Fable) found holes that are closed here:
- A link's `because:` text is part of what is signed but was not on the review page: now every signed field is shown, the parent and each link with what it says besides its target.
- The rules of a batch held only in the browser: `kea sign` now refuses, from any `Signs:` line, a result or a conclusion and, under `pace: design`, any page outside the question's design, and says which pages it did not sign.
- A step of another question that tests this question's hypothesis, and decisions under another question, were signed with this question's design: a page belongs to the question it sits under; the pages of the whole experiment are marked so.
- After the press the steps may still wait for pages elsewhere (another question's pages, a parent question, a questioned premise): the page lists them, and the message after signing says so instead of "the steps run at Implement".
- A page signed alone before the harvest got the batch mark when its design was signed too: a page the same reader has signed already, as it is, is kept as it was. Two sign-offs of one page in one harvest no longer lose the first.
- The question page's own Approve showed the design sign-off as its own and could withdraw all of it; a failed re-read of the page said the design had changed; another reader's press replaced the owner's button. All fixed.

Signatures from the stage bundles of 1.29-1.31.0 already on pages carry no mark (none of the current projects has one open).

- `kea context`, the skill, the CLAUDE.md section of `kea init` and Help (How a project progresses) name the button; Approve page by page stays.

Hub: upgrade not needed (page text and scripts; the sign-off is an ordinary Approve thread with a `Signs:` line, which the hub reads since 1.29; no endpoint, status field, event or publish format changed).
