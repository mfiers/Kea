## Asked

Mark Fiers, 9 November 2026: rename the hub roles to academic names (reader → auditor, commenter → reviewer, steerer → PI, owner → director) without breaking existing accounts; a page on the hub's own address for a signed-in reader (their projects, their role in each, pending requests, public projects, sign out) and one for visitors (what Kea and the hub are, Sign in with ORCID, the public projects); and, when the hub has ORCID, the shared-password form on a project's login page folded away under the ORCID button.

## Changed

- Roles are auditor < reviewer < PI < director (stored as `auditor`, `reviewer`, `pi`, `director`; shown as Auditor, Reviewer, PI, Director). The old names are read from an existing `users.json` and accepted by `kea hub users approve|role` (`steerer` → `pi`, also `PI`); the new names are written. The default role on approval is reviewer. `kea hub users` shows the role labels and the projects a reader asked for.
- The hub's own address (`https://<domain>/`) is a landing page instead of a line of text. A visitor sees the Kea head, two short paragraphs on Kea and the hub, a large Sign in with ORCID button and a table of public projects. A reader signed in on the hub sees their name and ORCID iD, a table of their projects (title and address, role with one line on what it may do, public or private, date of the last publish), the projects they asked to open that wait for approval, the other public projects, and Sign out. The hub serves the logo at `/kea-head.png`.
- Sign in on the hub itself: `/auth/login` without a return address goes to ORCID and back to the landing page. A reader who is not approved yet also gets the hub session, so their page can say what waits; it opens no project. A sign-in for a project without a role is recorded as a request.
- A project's login page on a hub with ORCID shows the ORCID button first and large; the shared-password form sits under "Sign in with a project password" (open after a wrong password). Without ORCID the form is unchanged.
- "Steerer" is now "PI" in the help texts of the cycle, the plans page, README, the skill, CLAUDE.md and its template.

## Checked

`pytest` (91 tests; new: role mapping and old names on the command line and in users.json, the visitor page with and without an ORCID client, the reader's page pending, after approval and after sign-out, the folded password form). The pages at 1280 px and 390 px in headless Firefox screenshots.
