## Asked

Mark Fiers, 18 November 2026, on a page showing "✓ Approve all 4 (S4, D2, D3, S5)": what four, do I need that? Then: make it simple; never approve all; approve and move to the next approval, top down, following the logic of the tree; navigation buttons are fine.

## Found

- One Approve signed a whole stage (`signing.bundle`, 1.29). Worse, every decision and premise that waited anywhere in the experiment was added to every bundle, so approving two steps also signed two unrelated decisions. The button showed only labels (S4, D2, D3, S5), in no clear order.

## Changed

- An Approve signs the page it is on, and nothing else. `signing.bundle` is gone; threads of 1.29-1.31.0 with a `Signs:` line are still applied by `kea sign`.
- New `signing.sign_order`: the pages that wait for a sign-off, top down through the tree (the experiment, its premises and decisions, then each question with its hypotheses, steps, results and conclusions; a page that fits nowhere comes after its parent). It is written to `_build.json`.
- On a page that waits: "✓ Approve & next →" signs it and opens the next unsigned page in that order; on the last page, "✓ Approve" goes to Progress with a note that the Harvest is next. "‹ n of N · k to sign ›" moves between the pages without signing.
- Progress: "Needs your sign-off" is one numbered list in the same order, with "Start signing at the top →" (it follows the first page still unsigned). The Progress count in the menu bar is the pages left to sign.
- Help, skill and tag instructions say one page per click.
