## Asked

Mark Fiers, 15 November 2026: an independent review (Fable 5.2) of how usable Kea is for biologists, with five plans and five long-range ideas; then: make suggestions for the problems found, fix what is easy, plan the rest, and start with plan 1 (binding signatures). There must be a way for minor edits to change a text without revoking an agreement, assessed by the agent; signing must be as painless as possible (a simple project must not need twenty sign-offs).

## Found

- A sign-off did not bind to what was signed: `graph.approved()` only checked that `approved:` existed, and the stored version was never compared with the page; a criterion rewritten after approval still showed "✓ approved".
- The agent wrote `approved:` by hand; nothing checked it against the comment log.
- Premises, decisions and conclusions never waited for a sign-off; a conclusion and the answer went out unsigned.
- Every page needed its own click: a question with three hypotheses and five steps was eight pages and eight clicks.

## Changed

- New `docsite/signing.py`. A signature carries `content:`, a hash of what the page says (title, summary, text and fields, without status, answer, bookkeeping fields and the links that say what the page goes on to support). `approved()` holds only while the page still says that, or while every edit since was recorded as minor. Progress (a step that runs, a hypothesis outcome, the answer) keeps the signature.
- A page edited after its sign-off waits again, with a "changed since <name> signed" pill and a note; this is not a build problem, so the site still builds and the owner can sign the new wording.
- Minor edits: `kea sign <id> --minor --because "…"`. The agent judges; Kea checks mechanically against the signed wording from git and refuses the edit as minor when a number, a negation or other meaning-bearing word (direction, quantifier, modal, causal), a link, a short or non-text field (certainty, relations, …) or more than max(5 words, 15%) changed. Minor edits show on the signature pill and their reasons on hover; Progress lists each signature's state.
- `kea sign` writes signatures (never by hand): `--thread T` applies a reader's Approve or Approve & go, bound to the version the reader saw, and the thread is checked against the comment log (a mismatch is a problem); `<id> --by NAME` records the owner's word in chat (shown as recorded by the agent); `--adopt` binds signatures made before 1.29; `--list` shows them all.
- One click per stage. Premises, decisions and conclusions now wait under `pace: guided`. Approve on a waiting page signs every page waiting in the same stage ("✓ Approve all 3: H1, S1, S2", listed before the confirmation; the thread records a `Signs:` line with each page's version); "✓ This page only" signs one. The stages are: the experiment with its proposed questions, premises and decisions; a question's plan; its results; its conclusions. A question costs about four clicks.
- The conclusions stage of the route waits for a sign-off, also on an answered question.
- Skill, tag instructions, help (Progress), CLAUDE.md template, README.

## Checked

`pytest` (127 tests; 6 new in `tests/test_signing.py`: what is signed, a signature that stops holding, legacy signatures, minor-or-not cases, bundles, `kea sign` with chat, minor, refused minor and a bundled site thread, and a thread naming someone else). Kea's own site builds with 0 problems. A guided demo project (scratch folder): the bundle data on a planned step, "Approve all 3 (H1, S1, S2)" on Progress, a bundled Approve applied with `kea sign --thread`, a minor edit carried, `0.05 → 0.1` refused as not minor, and the "changed since Ann signed" pill.

## Not done (planned)

- Signatures are still written into front matter by the agent (through `kea sign`); a site signature is checked against the local comment log, but the log is a file the agent can edit. Next: the hub records signature events itself and `kea check` compares against the hub's record (tamper-resistant only there).
- `confirmed:` (Confirm on settled pages) is not bound to the wording yet; plan: merge Confirm into Approve (plan 5) rather than bind a second mechanism.
- A question's `answer:` is not part of any signature; plan: the conclusions bundle includes a hash of the answer.
- A side-by-side diff of the signed and current wording on the page (History has the versions; no diff view yet).
- From the review, not started: plan 2 (one review screen per decision, step `plain:` and `decides:` fields), plan 3 (outcome scenarios signed before the data), plan 4 (digest and notifications to readers), plan 5 (plain-language reader mode: seven labels, three verdicts). `fixed_before_results` can now be checked: a hypothesis signed before the first result of its question was committed; not built.
- Not tried on a hub or in a browser with `kea serve` (the dock's "Approve all" and "This page only" buttons were checked only through the built HTML and `node --check`).
