## Asked

Mark Fiers, 19 November 2026: "can we get shortcuts when not in an input/text box? e.g. p > plan, g > progress (just suggestions, propose shortcuts)", then: "maybe a two step thing? e.g. g (for go) and then we see a quick popup menu that shows the options?"

## Changed

- g opens a small "Go to …" menu in the middle of the page with one key per place: s Start, e the experiment, u the page above, m Map, o Objects, r Progress, p Plan, c Current comments, h History, d Data files, k Code, ? Help. One more key goes there; Esc or any other key closes it; a row can also be clicked. Places the page has no link to are greyed out.
- Single keys on a page: c opens or closes the comments on this page; n and N go to the next and previous page to sign (the same as the dock's › and ‹, or Next to sign); ] and [ step through the tabs below the text, 1 to 9 open a tab by number; ? lists every shortcut, greying out those that do nothing on this page.
- Keys are ignored in text fields and with Ctrl, Alt or ⌘. No key signs, harvests or implements.
- Help (Reading a page) lists them. The shortcuts are in the site script (`_assets/kea.js`), so they also work on a site without a comment server (except c and n, which need the comment bar).

Hub: upgrade not needed (site script only).

## Checked

`pytest` (131 passed); `make site` (0 problems). Headless Chromium on `kea serve`, a guided scratch project: on Q1 g showed the menu and g m opened the experiment's map; g x stayed on the page and closed the menu; Esc closed it; ] opened Changes and 1 About this page; ? showed the list with N greyed out (Q1 is not in the sign order); c opened the comment window; typing "gm" in its text field typed the letters and stayed on the page; on Progress n opened D1, the first page to sign; g r opened Progress.
