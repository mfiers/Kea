## Asked

Mark Fiers, 19 November 2026: "I don't get the confirm button, confirm next plan? Also, I can't toggle it? Turn it off again, but then it is stuck until a hard reload. Is there a button to the next step that needs approving? Bottom foldout like next run, or manuscript fall off the screen on mobile."

## Found

- The stuck toggle was a bug in the request key that stops a resent request from being stored twice. The key was the page load plus the request body, so Confirm, take it back, Confirm again sent the same key as the first Confirm, and the server returned the old, withdrawn thread instead of storing a new one. Every toggle on a page hit this (Approve, Drop, Revise, the Next run and Manuscript chips) until the page was reloaded.
- On a settled page the button read "✓ Confirm", and once pressed "✓ Confirm · next plan", which reads as "confirm the next plan".
- The "‹ n of N ›" navigation and "Approve & next" showed only on a page that waits for a sign-off; elsewhere the only way to the next page to sign was Progress.
- The Next run, Manuscript, Revise and Propose popups open to the left of the dock; on a phone there is no room there, and they ran off the screen.

## Changed

- A request key is reused only while that request is under way or after it failed (a double click, a retry); once the server stored it, the same click is a new request with a new key.
- On a settled page the button is "✓ Keep as is" (it is right as it stands; Claude records the confirmation on the page at the next Harvest and changes nothing else). Pending verdicts read "· waits for Harvest" instead of "· next plan" (Keep as is, Withdrawn, Revise, Drop, and the page pills).
- Every page that does not itself wait, the project's front page included, has "✓ Next to sign (k) →" in the dock while pages wait for a sign-off; it opens the first unsigned page in the top-down order.
- On a phone (and wherever a popup would run off the left edge) the dock popups open as a sheet across the bottom of the screen, scrolling when long, with a × to close; a tap elsewhere closes it too.
- Help (How a project progresses, Reviewing) names Keep as is.

## Checked

`pytest` (130 passed after `make web`). Headless Chromium on `kea serve`, a guided scratch project: on Q1 (settled) the button went Keep as is → Keep as is · waits for Harvest → Keep as is → … over five clicks (before the fix the third click did nothing); "✓ Next to sign (4) →" showed on Q1 and on the front page and opened D1, where it is hidden and "Approve & next" shows. At 390 px wide, Next run, Manuscript and Propose open as a 374 px sheet at the bottom, inside the screen, and × closes each; at 1280 px they open beside the dock as before.
