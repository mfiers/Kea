## Asked

Mark Fiers, 27 November 2026: make sure Kea and the Kea hub are very mobile friendly; recheck, and push the updates (including trajectories).

## Found

Headless Chromium as a phone (320, 360 and 390 px wide, touch) on 26 pages of the trajectories site (graph, experiment, question with Sign this design, result, figure, step, conclusion, decision, objects, relations, map, plan, progress, comments, help, history, data, code, change log, cycles) and the hub's own pages (front page, help, not published yet), with the menu, the comment dock and Sign this design opened:

- The plan page scrolled sideways (1090 px wide on a 390 px phone): a plan line starting with several page links ("Q3 and Q6 and Q7 are named …") was turned into one link holding all of them, which cannot wrap.
- In the menu bar the home logo was squeezed to a 3 px sliver when the round's button had a long label ("Read the plan · 6 items").
- On the hub's front and help pages at 320 px the menu bar was 9 px too wide.
- Everything else fitted: no sideways scroll, tables scroll inside their own box, tapping a page in the graph opens it full width.

## Changed

- A list item is joined into one tag-and-text link only when it starts with exactly one page link (render.TAGLINE_ITEM).
- The menu bar keeps 28 px for the home logo.
- Hub pages on phones: tighter menu bar; below 400 px the version pill is hidden.

Hub: upgrade not needed for sites (page style and rendering); the hub's own pages get the narrower menu bar when it is upgraded.

## Checked

`pytest` (184 passed; tests/test_mobile.py new). A rebuilt trajectories site and a local hub: no page wider than the phone at 320, 360 or 390 px; the logo is 24 px wide at 320 px.

Not checked: the hub's front page when signed in with ORCID (the project table), and real devices (iOS Safari, Android Chrome).
