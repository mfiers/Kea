## Asked

Mark Fiers, 20 November 2026: "Can you ensure that if a text changes (more than a typo) everything downstream needs to be re-signed off, at the least! … a minor change (e.g. typo, a harmless edit) or a major change, which needs individual sign off for daughter pages. If that happens, provide a reason why that page needs reassessment by showing what changed upstream. Lastly it could be a dramatic change which invalidates (deletes?) anything downstream. … it is of utmost importance to maintain a correct mental image of the project to the user." And: "it should be easy to delete a branch. E.g. simply remove or change a question and force everything downstream removed (maybe keep reusable bits around) to redo parts of an experiment with better understanding."

Design reviewed with a second model (Fable); Mark chose: everything downstream stops counting, cleared top down; an invalidating change retires the branch (kept as a record); a question whose ask changes becomes a new page that supersedes the old one; the agent proposes the class in the plan, Mark decides at Implement.

## Found

- A substantive change voided only the changed page's own signature. The pages below kept theirs; they were only flagged for the agent's recheck, which the agent cleared by itself.
- Worse: the recheck graph had no edge from a page to the page it sits under. In trajectories nothing depended on q-confounders, so rewriting it flagged nothing at all.

## Changed

- `propagation.py` (new): a sign-off counts only with what the page was built on when it was signed. Built on: the page it sits under (for a question, hypothesis, step, decision, premise, caveat), the pages it relies on (supported_by, opposed_by, relies_on, made_by, from_artefact, derived_from, decided_in, addressed_by), a step's hypothesis (tests), a conclusion's results (supports/opposes). Not terms, references, resources; not a result on the question it answers.
- `kea sign` writes `upstream: {page: content hash}` into each new signature; for older signatures the hashes are read from git at the commit that added the signature.
- After a substantive change, signed pages directly built on the changed page wait for their own sign-off; signed pages further down are held ("signed by Mark · Q1 waits first"): they need no click and count again by themselves once the pages above are signed again unchanged. Minor edits recorded on a signature that holds change nothing below. A page is never offered for signing while a page above it waits.
- A waiting page shows a box: which page changed, the recorded reason, and a word diff of that page since this one was signed. Progress says how many pages wait because of an upstream change and how many are held.
- `kea change <id> --because "…"` records why a signed page changed (doc/_checks.yaml, `changes:`); the build fails on a signed page changed beyond a minor edit without one. A tested hypothesis whose prediction or criterion changed is a build problem: that is a new, post hoc hypothesis.
- `kea impact <id>`: what a change would make wait and hold, and what retiring would remove and keep (for the plan).
- `kea drop <id> --apply --because "…" [--replaced-by <id>]` retires the page and what rests only on it (`retired:` in the front matter). Retired pages leave the map, Progress, the sign order and counts; their page stays, marked retired, listed under Map → Retired; links to them still work, marked retired; a relation from a live page to a retired one (other than supersedes) is a problem. Premises, decisions, resources, references and terms under a retired page are kept and moved to the replacing page; artefacts a live page uses are kept.
- The recheck ledger now also flags the pages under a changed page (its direct children; nothing is passed on through a parent, as a question also depends on its results).
- Skill (sign-offs: substantive, invalidating, what a change does below it; the plan names the class and `kea impact`), the CLAUDE.md block, help (How a project progresses).

Hub: upgrade not needed (computed at build; no endpoint or status field changed). Minor release because it changes what a sign-off means.

## Checked
